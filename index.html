The Broke Student's Cheat Code: Unlock the Free Perks You're Already Paying For

You're already paying for perks most students never use. Here's how to claim them in one afternoon.

Start at Step 1 Jump to the Perk Finder

Project goal

Help college students claim valuable student perks in one afternoon. We walk you through 7 short steps. Each step tells you what you need, what to click, and what to watch out for. By the end, you'll have discounted music, shopping, tech tools, and news, plus a tracker so nothing charges you by surprise.

Who this is for

This guide is for college students who want to save money. Steps 1–4 and Step 7 work for most U.S. college students. Steps 5 and 6 are only for Syracuse University students. They are marked SU only.

The 7 steps at a glance

Follow them in order, or jump to the one you need.

  1. Gather what you need
  2. Get Spotify Premium Student (with Hulu included)
  3. Start Amazon Prime Student
  4. Claim the GitHub Student Developer Pack
  5. Activate free NYT, Wall Street Journal, and Washington Post SU only
  6. Use Adobe Creative Cloud free SU only
  7. Track everything so nothing charges you by surprise
