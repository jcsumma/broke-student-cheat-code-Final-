Entertainment Perks

Steps 2–3 of 7

Step 2: Get Spotify Premium Student (with Hulu included)

Pay $6.99/month instead of $12.99/month for regular Premium Individual. Premium Student is for eligible U.S. college students and includes Hulu (With Ads) at no extra cost.

You'll need: your school email and student info from Step 1.

  1. Go to spotify.com/student.
  2. Sign up for Premium Student.
  3. Verify your student status.
  4. Activate Hulu from your Spotify account page.

⚠️ Watch out: The discount lasts up to 4 years total, and you must re-verify every year.

Source: Spotify Premium Student official page

Step 3: Start Amazon Prime Student (Prime for Young Adults)

Get a 6-month free trial as a new member. After that, pay $7.49/month or $69/year instead of $14.99/month or $139/year for regular Prime. You get free delivery on eligible items, Prime Video, Amazon Music Prime, Kindle Unlimited, and Grubhub+.

You'll need: your school email and your phone's calendar.

  1. Go to Amazon's Prime Student page.
  2. Verify your student status.
  3. Start the free trial.
  4. Set a calendar reminder for one week before the trial ends. Do it right away.

⚠️ Watch out: If you've already had a Prime trial, you may not get another. Prime Student converts to full-price Prime after graduation or at age 25 unless you cancel.

Source: Amazon Prime Student official page
