Step 4: Claim the GitHub Student Developer Pack

Get free GitHub Pro while you're a student, plus rotating partner tools. It's free for students 13 and older enrolled in a degree program. It's useful for portfolios and personal websites, not just coding.

You'll need: your @syr.edu email and your student ID.

  1. Create a GitHub account.
  2. Go to education.github.com/students.
  3. Apply with your @syr.edu email and student ID.
  4. Wait for approval.
  5. Browse the current partner offers.

💡 Tip: Partner offers change, so check what's currently included.

Source: GitHub Education for students
