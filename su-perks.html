Syracuse University Perks

Steps 5–6 of 7

These two steps are only for current Syracuse University students. Not at SU? Skip ahead to Step 7: Track everything.

Step 5: Activate free NYT, Wall Street Journal, and Washington Post SU only

Read the news for free through SU Libraries. Current students with @syr.edu emails get free digital New York Times and Wall Street Journal access, plus an online Washington Post subscription. You also get access past the Syracuse.com paywall.

You'll need: your @syr.edu email and your graduation year.

  1. Go to the SU Libraries New York Times guide.
  2. Follow its registration link.
  3. Sign up with your @syr.edu email and enter your graduation year.
  4. Repeat using the Libraries' Wall Street Journal and Washington Post instructions.

⚠️ Watch out: The NYT student account expires in December of the graduation year you enter. You can't use the same @syr.edu email for both a personal NYT account and the Libraries account.

Sources: SU Libraries NYT research guide and SU News: Libraries license The Washington Post

Step 6: Use Adobe Creative Cloud free SU only

Use Adobe Creative Cloud at no cost. All SU students can use it on ITS lab computers and through SU Remote Desktop. Architecture, Newhouse, and some VPA students get personal-device licenses during semesters they're in qualifying courses.

You'll need: your SU email and password, and a campus lab computer or SU Remote Desktop.

  1. Open an Adobe app on a campus lab computer or through SU Remote Desktop.
  2. Choose to sign in with a company or school account.
  3. Use your SU email and password.
  4. Check your school's page for a personal-device license if you're in Architecture, Newhouse, or a qualifying VPA course.

Source: SU ITS Answers: Adobe at Syracuse University
