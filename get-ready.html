Step 1: Gather what you need

Set yourself up before you start. Having everything in one place makes the rest go fast.

You'll need: your student ID, your @syr.edu email and NetID login, a personal email, a phone, and about one hour.

  1. Grab your student ID card.
  2. Log in to your @syr.edu email with your NetID so you can open verification emails.
  3. Open your personal email in another tab.
  4. Keep your phone nearby for codes and calendar reminders.
  5. Block off about one hour.

💡 Tip: Keep this guide open in one tab and your school email in another. Many sign-ups send a verification link to your inbox.
