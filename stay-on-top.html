Step 7: Track everything so nothing charges you by surprise

Protect your savings. Free trials and student discounts can turn into full-price charges if you forget about them.

You'll need: your phone and the tracker below.

  1. List every perk you claimed.
  2. Note the trial end or renewal date for each one.
  3. Set a phone reminder one week before each date.
  4. Re-verify your student status every year.

⚠️ Watch out: Amazon Prime Student converts to full-price Prime after graduation or at age 25 unless you cancel. See Step 3.
